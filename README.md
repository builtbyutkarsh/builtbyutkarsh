## 💫 About Me

I'm a founder and business-oriented technologist interested in understanding how markets work, why people make decisions, and how technology can solve meaningful problems at scale.

- **Currently working on:** Building Heavohealth and exploring how technology can improve real-world healthcare outcomes.
- **Looking to collaborate on:** Healthcare innovation, AI-powered products, emerging technologies, and early-stage startups.
- **Looking for help with:** Scaling products, building distribution engines, improving go-to-market strategies, and turning ideas into sustainable businesses.
- **Currently learning:** AI, growth strategy, product thinking, blockchain, and scalable systems.
- **Ask me about:** Startups, go-to-market strategy, growth, founder's office, business models, and healthcare innovation.
- **Fun fact:** I'm more interested in building systems that scale than ideas that sound good.

### What I Think About

- **Strategy:** Finding opportunities where customer needs, market dynamics, and business models intersect.
- **Growth & Distribution:** Understanding how products reach the right people and turn demand into sustainable growth.
- **Product Thinking:** Connecting customer problems to product decisions and measurable outcomes.
- **Technology:** Exploring how AI, software, and blockchain create leverage for businesses and people.
- **Human Behaviour:** Studying psychology, incentives, game theory, and decision-making.

I believe the most interesting problems rarely belong to one discipline. Solving them requires understanding the customer, the business, the technology, and the system connecting them.

---

## 🧠 How I Approach Problems

I like to start with the problem, understand the incentives, question the assumptions, and work backwards towards a solution.

My interests span:

- First-principles thinking
- Market dynamics and business models
- Go-to-market strategy and distribution
- Customer psychology and behavioural economics
- Product strategy and systems thinking
- AI-powered workflows and emerging technology

**The goal isn't to use technology for its own sake. It's to find where technology creates meaningful leverage.**
